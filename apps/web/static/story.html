Our story

It started with a kitchen in Poka.

GLS opened in 2018 with a simple idea: serve good food, bake fresh bread and create a place people are happy to return to. Today there are two GLS locations, and the idea hasn't changed.

Good food should bring people together. And a meal doesn't need an occasion to be worth enjoying.
